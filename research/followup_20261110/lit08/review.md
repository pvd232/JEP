# Spanish, Colombian and cultural validity: evidence and defensible gap
Review cutoff: 2026-11-10. Eight primary research bodies and one official JEP institutional source were inspected. This is a bounded systematic scoping review, not proof of exhaustive absence. Methods and limitations are logged in evidence_ledger.json; uninspected leads remain excluded.

## Finding
The attractive broad novelty claims are already false: JEP hearing text mining, Colombian expert legal evaluation, Spanish legal embeddings, cultural variation benchmarks and multilingual theory-conditioned moral training all have direct predecessors. The defensible hypothesis is narrower: **holding the textual scenario and named moral concept fixed, explicit theory/value conditioning improves expert-judged retrieval of source-supported implicit individual, collective and territorial harm, while preserving semantic invariances and representing disagreement or insufficiency.** Whether this gap is globally novel remains uncertain; whether it is materially relevant to JEP has strong institutional support.

## Closest prior work and what it does not settle
1. [Sosa Martinez et al., 2026, DOI10.15446/rce.v49n1.119765](https://doi.org/10.15446/rce.v49n1.119765), §3 and regional/role results: an actual Case03 hearing corpus includes Casanare and linguistic networks. It decisively defeats “first JEP NLP/network visualization.” Its descriptive lexical analysis is not a source-validated benchmark of conditional harm/reparation relevance.
2. [Colombian-law reliability benchmark, arXiv2610.03639](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03639), §§3–4: Colombian expert-reviewed legal QA is already available. Inspecting the authoring process shows why expert provenance alone is insufficient: independent duplicate coding and measured agreement are separate requirements. No retrieval result is inferred from its legal QA design.
3. [MEL, arXiv2501.16011v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.16011v1), §§3–4: legal Spanish continued pretraining has classification evidence. This does not establish transfer to Colombian oral testimony or morally conditioned similarity.
4. [MultiLegalPile, ACL2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.805/), §§3–4: multilingual legal encoders/corpora provide necessary comparison baselines. Their existing classification/entity benchmarks answer a different question from culturally grounded victim-harm relevance.
5. [RoBERTalex,2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.12201), §§2–6: Spanish legal embeddings and a legal transformer are established. Its general-task comparison does not warrant assuming legal-domain pretraining improves similarity; model-selection differences further qualify the comparison.
6. [BLEnD,NeurIPS2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.09948), §3 andTable1: locally authored cultural evaluation, including different Spanish-speaking countries, precedes this proposal. Colombia is outside its country table. Everyday cultural knowledge is not collective-harm interpretation; a language code cannot serve as a single cultural preference.
7. [MET/MET-D,2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11736), §§3–4: adaptive theoretical grounds, culturally adapted Spanish moral scenarios and synthetic self-distillation directly precede proposed theory conditioning. Its synthetic character priorities are an explicitly different supervision source from victims' or practitioners' judgments. “Use MET on Spanish” is replication/application, not a novelty claim.
8. [Legal-ES,2020](https://aclanthology.org/2020.lt4gov-1.6/), §§2–4: Spanish legal embeddings, topic-based navigation and preliminary graph communities are older still. Visually coherent legal clusters cannot substitute for held-out judgments.

The cross-lane SCS2026 Spanish Amazon conditional-clustering result is an additional counterexample reported by lit01; it was not independently body-inspected in this lane and is not counted among the eight.

## JEP-specific semantic requirement
The official [JEP Case09 opening communiqué,13September2022](https://www.jep.gov.co/Sala-de-Prensa/Paginas/JEP-abre-caso-09-investigar-crimenes-contra-pueblos-etnicos.aspx) treats collective/territorial subjects, sacred relations, disrupted cultural transmission and intergenerational consequences as material context. This institutional source supplies normative and contextual constraints; it supplies no model score or empirical validation. A schema limited to individual suffering or net utility can therefore omit the target construct. A universal “harm vector” or “Spanish morality” would be an unsupported simplification.

## Decisive experiment for this lane
Build a small, source-verified Spanish evaluation set, with JEP practitioner and relevant community review obtained rather than presumed. Evaluation units must retain hearing/video/timecode and enough surrounding context. Include explicit versus implicit harm; individual, household, community and territorial referents; current and intergenerational effects; and cases in which the passage gives insufficient evidence. Preserve annotator-level relevance judgments, stated interpretation and disagreement. These are design requirements, not claims that annotation has been performed.

For the same harm concept and same scenario, compare: ordinary semantic retrieval; legal-register baseline; fixed concept conditioning; concept plus explicitly specified theory/value priorities; and theory-swapped conditions. Keep source facts fixed across conditions. Do not assume a legal instruction, community standpoint and consequentialist/deontological theory are interchangeable condition labels. Report each separately.

Hold out scenario/hearing families and paraphrase families together. Use independent human relevance labels on held-outs; do not tune prompts, rank thresholds or geometry on them. Synthetic paraphrases may test robustness but their expected labels remain synthetic. Report nDCG/Recall, source-entailment error, unsupported cultural inference, abstention calibration, annotator-stratified agreement and the interaction between theory condition and collective-harm category. Pair each theoretical change with a positive-control distinction expected to matter and negative-control wording changes expected not to matter.

A directional harm-to-restoration relation should be evaluated as typed adequacy/response relevance, not forced into symmetric similarity: a proposed response may address only one component of a multi-component harm. Test both query directions and intervention coverage. A shared inner-product embedding is inherently symmetric; good symmetric retrieval would not by itself establish reciprocal restorative adequacy or moral reasoning.

## Gap judgment
- **High confidence:** direct JEP NLP and Spanish legal/multilingual moral predecessors forbid broad absence/first claims.
- **Moderate confidence:** the inspected evidence does not establish expert-validated, source-grounded, theory-conditional retrieval of implicit collective/territorial harm in JEP hearings. This is an evidence gap within the inspected set, not an exhaustive world-first claim.
- **Low confidence:** a particular restorative embedding symmetry is novel or normatively appropriate. Recent conceptual/ecological justice literature remains a required citation chase, and symmetry may be the wrong construct.

Recommendation: **pursue the bounded evaluation question; revise the novelty and label design.** Use existing encoders/outputs. Keep the cleaned CSTS arm as a conditional-semantics calibration task, separate from moral/JEP validity; it cannot validate community moral meaning. Obtain actual human-cultural validation before making deployment or epistemic claims. Negative results should identify which relation/condition fails, not trigger test-label-driven prompt revision.

## Open leads and scope limits
The 2025 paper “Transitional justice and other-than-human harm: lessons from Colombia” (DOI10.1080/13642987.2025.2502561) and the 2026 conceptual Macrocase05 paper “Communities in restorative justice” (DOI10.3389/fsoc.2026.1936377) were discovered but not body-inspected here because of access restrictions. They preclude dismissing ecological/collective justice as unexplored. No contemporary numerical performance claim is made from uninspected result tables. Local PDFs are reading copies, not publication artifacts.
