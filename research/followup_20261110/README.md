## What actually ran

The corrected fixture has 12 authored hypothetical incident families, 84 response candidates and 48 queries. Six families are held out. Each identical incident passage and seven-candidate pool is ranked under two explicit criteria: completed typed response for the named party versus acknowledgment/voice. Proposed action, unknown completion, another beneficiary, sanction and non-repetition are separate distractors. All have supportive wording. Passive reformulations retain the explicit agent.

No training occurred. Frozen TF-IDF controls were executed. On 24 test queries, query-only Recall@1 was 0.333; adding the criterion gave 0.750. Both criterion rankings were strictly correct on 0.000 versus 0.667 of test switch pairs. This shows the fixture exposes criterion sensitivity and a strong lexical shortcut; it does not establish a neural, moral-reasoning or JEP-utility improvement. The structured-attribute oracle reads authored metadata and is only an upper bound. The benchmark remains English and synthetic, without independent human labels.

The exact restored Qwen adapter and pinned backbone were prepared with 324 cached-context tasks; maximum input length was 88 tokens including EOD under the 160-token training limit. Spot and standard L4 capacity admissions failed across attempted zones. No follow-up GPU was allocated and no Qwen predictions were produced. The missing arm is capacity-blocked, not a negative scientific result. No alternative encoder was substituted, no backbone was trained, and no successful original-pilot stages were rerun. The original overnight pilot and its completed results are preserved.

## Scientific implications

Generic moral spaces, conditional similarity, invariance augmentation, rationale-to-encoder transfer and layer probing all have close predecessors. There is also actual JEP Case03 text-mining prior work and a close restorative-court-parsing proposal. These must be cited rather than bypassed with broad novelty claims.

The strongest next empirical target is Spanish source-grounded retrieval with explicit beneficiary, evidence, completion status and uncertainty. Harm-to-response is directed and many-to-many; completed repair is not an algebraic inverse of harm. Theory-conditioned judgments require an operationalized theory and independently assessed relevance, kept separate from synthetic expectations and LLM labels. The present diagnostic tests two response criteria, not ethical theories.
