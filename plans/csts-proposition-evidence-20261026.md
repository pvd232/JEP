# Proposition generation supervision for Qwen

The experiment tests whether source-grounded generated propositions improve Qwen conditional similarity after rating supervision. It uses Qwen/Qwen3-Embedding-8B at c90816d848505624c2434128dfc61132162a0ee9 and the existing frozen Qwen3-8B-Base vocabulary head.

Both arms start with identical backbone and LoRA initialization. Phase A uses the same 2,303 original training comparisons, identical permutations, batch32/microbatch2 and 144 optimizer updates over two epochs. The control minimizes rating cosine MSE. The proposition arm minimizes causal answer-token cross entropy. Input token counts and elapsed compute are recorded separately. Phase B restores terminal phase-A adapters and starts fresh optimizer/RNG state. Both arms receive all6,980 rating comparisons at constant learning rate2e-5, rank/alpha32, dropout.05, patience5 and a24-epoch ceiling. Phase-A update counts are matched; phase-B stopping follows the same validation rule.

The existing source-only GPT-6.1-sol/high bank supplies2,880 captured training inputs, including2,486 answered targets. Available capture and teacher answerability determine auxiliary coverage. The frozen policy passed a model-assisted58/60 fidelity gate. Its inference policy is stricter than Tu for some attributes. This experiment retains that policy and records its provenance. Alltargettexts fit the unchanged64-token ceiling; the longest is36 tokens including EOD. Each target is checked against its original source and exact quotations. Training captions/groups and validation captions/groups remain disjoint.

Validation is the frozen v4.1 audit:1,572 rows/678 groups, SHA256 f186e86877e731c96ee9b1c819863b981074bc500dc9aa11c6951ba9fd56e2c9. Both selected checkpoints freeze before scoring the2,620 Tu human-reannotated test comparisons. Training labels stay at the existing6,980-row release version. Raw Spearman is primary. Validation-fitted positive affine calibration supplies rating-scale errors; it preserves raw rank calculations. Overall, appearance and lexical colour cohorts receive paired source-group intervals. Subgroups are exploratory and training uses one seed.

Execution holds the existing shared GPU lease. Native stages seal locally and queue cloud publication asynchronously. The source, data identities, traces, selection, prediction artifacts and report are retained.
